# GAP-010: Corporate Contributor Authorization

- Status: Open — activates when the first organizational contribution appears
- Owner: Maintainer

## Unknown

Whether an employer or other organization controls rights in a proposed contribution and what authorization evidence or corporate agreement is necessary.

## Why It Does Not Block Now

No organizational contribution has been identified. Creating a Corporate CLA workflow before a real case would add unnecessary process.

## Evidence Needed to Close

A real organizational contribution, the contributor's employment/authorization context, and legal review of whether employer authorization or a Corporate CLA is required.
