# GAP-009: Garmin Unofficial Provider Terms

- Status: Open — blocks stable commercial reliance on an unofficial provider
- Owner: Maintainer

## Unknown

The selected provider's license and provenance, and whether its use complies with applicable Garmin service, API, data-use, redistribution, and contractual terms.

## Why It Does Not Block Unrelated Work

Core boundaries and provider-replacement design can proceed without selecting or distributing an unofficial provider. No Garmin provider is implemented yet.

## Evidence Needed to Close

Identify the actual provider, review its authoritative license and provenance, review applicable Garmin terms for the intended behavior, and evaluate official Garmin access as the preferred long-term path. Stable commercial use requires a separate documented review.
