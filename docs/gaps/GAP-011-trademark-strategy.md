# GAP-011: Project Trademark Strategy

- Status: Open — non-blocking
- Owner: Maintainer

## Unknown

Whether `universal-data-mcp-worker` will remain only the technical repository name or whether a separate protectable commercial brand will be introduced.

## Why It Does Not Block Now

The current short trademark policy prevents misleading affiliation and separates third-party marks without requiring a formal trademark program.

## Evidence Needed to Close

Actual public adoption, commercial offerings, branding use, collision searches, and professional legal advice if registration or enforcement becomes worthwhile.
